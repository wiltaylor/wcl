# wplan — processes

Each process is a runbook on its own page. This is the index.

- [**Starting a plan**](../references/process_proc_starting_a_plan.md) — Place and scaffold the verified template, and prove it checks before any content goes in.

- [**Running the interview**](../references/process_proc_interview.md) — Settle every decision an implementation agent could stumble on - by the user, in writing - before anything else happens.

- [**Doing the research**](../references/process_proc_research.md) — Leave nothing for implementation agents to figure out - weak models doing mid-build research is the primary failure mode this pipeline exists to prevent.

- [**Writing the PRD**](../references/process_proc_write_prd.md) — Capture goals, non-goals, prioritised requirements and the universal project rules, then configure the gates.

- [**Breaking down the specs**](../references/process_proc_spec_breakdown.md) — Produce the DAG of parallel-safe, weak-model-proof specs.

- [**Analyzing the plan**](../references/process_proc_analyze_plan.md) — A judgment pass over the whole plan before rendering - catching what gates cannot express.

- [**Rendering and handoff**](../references/process_proc_render_handoff.md) — Produce both projections and give each role exactly what it needs.

- [**Verifying and reviewing a spec**](../references/process_proc_verify_spec.md) — The verification agent's protocol: judge an implementation, record the verdict, control the merge.
